# Justos

> Justos is a Brazilian digital auto insurance insurtech (founded 2020, Barueri, São Paulo) that operates as a managing general agent and uses smartphone-collected telematics to reward safe drivers with usage-based, lower-cost auto insurance. Products are distributed through a consumer mobile app and a broker portal. No public developer API or OpenAPI is published at this time.
